Exterior architectural visualization

(Overview)

Images that place the building in its real context — light, vegetation, scale and street — before a single brick is laid.

(What you get)
  • Photorealistic stills for print and web
  • Day, dusk or night scenes
  • Photo-integration into the real site
  • Landscape, vegetation and people at the right scale
  • Street-level and aerial cameras
  • Colour and material studies
(Useful for)
  • Planning and competition submissions
  • Pre-sales and marketing of developments
  • Presentations to clients and neighbours
  • Design reviews between architect and developer

01Can you work from a drone photo or site photograph?

Yes. A photograph of the real plot lets me match light and perspective so the building sits naturally in its surroundings.

02How many images does a project usually need?

Often two to four exterior views: a main one at eye level, an aerial or approach view and one alternative or detail. We settle this in the brief.
