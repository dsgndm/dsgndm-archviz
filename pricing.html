(Process)

How we
work

01

Brief & documents

Plans, material specification, location and references. We agree which images are needed and in what order.

02

Modelling & materials

I prepare the 3D model, cameras and finishes. Framings are approved before anything is rendered.

03

Light & test renders

Lighting and atmosphere are tuned through low-resolution tests you can react to early.

04

Delivery & revisions

Final render and retouching. Two revision rounds are included; more may adjust the price.

(Included)

Every project includes

  • Direct contact with me from the first reference to the final delivery
  • Framings approved before the final renders
  • Two revision rounds
  • High-resolution files ready for print and web
(What I need from you)
  • Plans (DWG or PDF), elevations and sections
  • Material and finish specification
  • Location and, if possible, a site photograph or drone view
  • References for mood, furniture or landscaping
  • Deadline and where the images will be used
(Price factors)

What changes
the price

Number of images

Each additional view shares the same model and materials, so it costs less than the first.

Model and complexity

A ready 3D model saves time. Complex geometry, landscaping or furnishing adds it.

Photo-integration

Placing the building in a real photograph needs the photo and a site reference.

Deadline

Tight schedules are possible but may change the price. Tell me the date at the start.

(FAQ)

Good
questions

01What do you need to get started?

Plans, the materials specification, the location and, if available, a reference of the surroundings such as a photo or drone view. With that I can prepare a closed proposal.

02How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of images and their complexity. Every proposal includes a week-by-week schedule with a delivery date for each milestone.

03How many revision rounds are included?

Two rounds are included in the price. If more are needed we talk before continuing, so there are no surprises.

04Which formats do you deliver?

High-resolution images ready for print or web, and Full HD video. If you need another size or format, tell me at the start.

05Can you start from sketches or an early design?

Yes. For early-stage images a clear massing model, or even sketches with dimensions, can be enough. We agree the level of detail in the brief so the image matches the stage of the project.

06How do we review the work?

Previews arrive as private links or PDFs with numbered views, so every comment can point to a specific image. Final files are delivered through a download link.

07What if the design changes after approval?

Corrections are part of the revision rounds. Changes to the design itself mean rebuilding parts of the model, so I quote them separately and tell you before doing any extra work.

08Do you work with architects and developers?

Yes. I usually collaborate with architecture studios, developers and interior designers: they bring the project and I turn it into images.
