(Principles)

How I
see it

Four ideas that guide every image, whatever the size of the project.

01

Light first

Light decides how a space feels. I tune it early, with test renders, before polishing anything else.

02

Honest materials

Finishes are modelled and lit to read as they will in reality, not as an idealised version of them.

03

Calm composition

Framings follow the architect’s intent, with camera heights chosen the way people will actually see the building.

04

Direct contact

One conversation from the first reference to the final delivery, with clear milestones and no surprises.

(Workflow)

How I
work

Process & pricing →
01

Brief & documents

Plans, material specification, location and references. We agree which images are needed and in what order.

02

Modelling & materials

I prepare the 3D model, cameras and finishes. Framings are approved before anything is rendered.

03

Light & test renders

Lighting and atmosphere are tuned through low-resolution tests you can react to early.

04

Delivery & revisions

Final render and retouching. Two revision rounds are included; more may adjust the price.
